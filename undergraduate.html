Undergraduate

Samarth pathways for students planning after high school.

Edufinite's undergraduate support is aimed at one of the most difficult decision points in a student's life: deciding what to study, where to study it, and how to avoid a choice that costs years of momentum.

Aptitude and fit Destination strategy Profile development

Plan Type Services Cost (INR)
Samarth-1Basic Basic Recommendation of up to 30 universities plus support for application forms to up to 7 colleges based on interest, marks, and finances. 30,000
Samarth-2Advanced Advanced Adds preparation for mandatory tests and additional course-specific requirements, with form support for up to 20 colleges. 50,000
Samarth-3Special Case Special Case Adds a broader personality package and deeper support intended for students who need more structured development. 3,00,000
Samarth-4All Stages All Stages Adds psychological testing, field suggestions, and the most comprehensive end-to-end support for early-stage planning. 5,00,000
The first consultation is free. After that, three consultations are charged at INR 1,000 each before a candidate continues with a selected service track.
Why undergraduate support matters

One wrong course can cost 3 to 5 years.

The reference material frames this stage as foundational. Students and families are often weighing aptitude, affordability, future-proof careers, geopolitics, and destination viability all at once.

Fields covered

Eight major academic directions.

  • STEM
  • Medical and health sciences
  • Commerce and management
  • Arts, humanities, and social sciences
  • Creative and media fields
  • Law and literature
  • Emerging careers including environment and sports management
Included Support

Preparation can extend far beyond university selection.

Depending on the chosen track, Edufinite may support test readiness for IELTS, TOEFL, or SAT-style requirements, extracurricular and profile positioning, and the broader hand-holding needed for a more credible undergraduate application.

How to start

  • Begin with the free consultation
  • Share marks, interests, and financial constraints
  • Select the Samarth level that matches the depth of support required
