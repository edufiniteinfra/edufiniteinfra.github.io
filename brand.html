01 — Logo

Primary Logo

The Edufinite wordmark uses a combination of bold and light weights. "EDU" is set in Bold (700) while "FINITE" uses Light weight (300).

EDUFINITE Light background
EDUFINITE Dark background
EDUFINITE Accent background

Clear Space

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "E" on all sides.

Minimum Size

Never use the logo smaller than 100px wide for digital or 25mm for print.
