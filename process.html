Process

How public inquiries turn into structured support.

The process begins with an inquiry, continues with a fit conversation, and then moves into structured guidance around planning, applications, readiness, and ongoing coordination.

How support unfoldsInquiry, consultation, program fit, and then deeper execution.
1

Inquiry and profile capture

A student or institution reaches out through the website or a shared registration route and provides baseline contact information.

2

Consultation setup

The first consultation is free, and the right level of ongoing support is defined after the initial fit discussion.

3

Program delivery

Once a pathway is selected, Edufinite coordinates the next steps with a more structured plan for execution and follow-through.

Response window

Expect a reply within 24 hours.

Queries are typically answered within 24 hours, and consultations can usually be scheduled within 24 to 72 hours.

Communication

Email is the formal channel.

Registered email is treated as the main communication route, with lighter follow-up also happening through WhatsApp where needed.

Students

Before sign-up

Students can begin with discovery, profile understanding, and consultation before choosing a Samarth or Saksham pathway.

Advisory team

After sign-up

Once a pathway is selected, the advisory team coordinates the process more directly while keeping communication clear and timely.

Delivery

Structured support after sign-up

Once a candidate selects a program, the service becomes more organized around execution, documents, and ongoing coordination.

Policies

Refund and payment expectations are explained before full engagement.

Payment structure, refund expectations, and any special circumstances are clarified before a candidate or partner moves into a paid support pathway.

Quick process notes

  • First consultation free
  • Scope shared after the first-fit conversation
  • Detailed break-up shared after track selection
